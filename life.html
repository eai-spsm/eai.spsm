A day here

The times, the rooms and what to know for a day at school.

  • 07:45

    Homeroom

    Later than this is late.

  • 11:20–12:55

    Lunch

    Go early. The lines are packed by 11:20.

  • 18:00–19:00

    Late days

    Hardware projects can run late. Tell your parents; transport is crammed.

  • Every term

    40 periods a week

    The same load every term, with credits from 14.0 to 15.5 a semester.
