What you will learn

1 / 8Scroll to pull the next sheet

Basic Programming

Learn to code in Python, from your first lines to a small app.

  • Weekly exercisesPython practice from W01 to W11, one topic a week.
  • Core PythonVariables, loops, functions and debugging.
  • Terminal ATM examFinal exam: build a working ATM program in the terminal.
  • Tkinter study plannerCapstone: a desktop app that plans your study time.

Microcontroller

Wire circuits and program boards that sense and move.

  • Basic circuitsOhm's law, series and parallel circuits, breadboard basics.
  • Arduino and GPIOProgram boards to read inputs and control outputs.
  • PWM and motorsDim lights and drive a servo with PWM.
  • Sensors and displayMeasure distance with ultrasound and show it on an I2C LCD.

Physics in Robotics

Build a robot that drives and follows the physics.

  • ESP32 robotBuild a differential-drive robot controlled by an ESP32.
  • CAD designModel the robot's parts in Onshape.
  • Motor protocolControl Dynamixel motors over their own protocol.

Chatbot

Make a LINE chatbot that answers real questions.

  • Dialogflow intentsDefine what users ask and how the bot answers.
  • Apps Script webhookConnect LINE to your own logic.
  • Sheets databaseStore and look up answers in Google Sheets.
  • Flex Message cardsReply with rich, designed cards.

Computer Vision

Teach a computer to see images and video.

  • Image processingFilter and transform images with OpenCV.
  • Face and hand detectionFind faces and hands in a live camera feed.
  • YOLO detectionDetect and label objects in real time.

Microcontroller and AI

Run AI on a small board in the real world.

  • Raspberry Pi labsVision and GPIO on a single-board computer.
  • Hand trackingFollow a hand from the camera.
  • Servo controlMove a servo based on what the camera sees.
  • YOLOv8 detectionRun object detection on the board.

Deep Learning

See how neural networks learn, then train your own.

  • AI vs ML vs DLHow the three differ and fit together.
  • Neural network anatomyLayers, weights and activations.
  • Model familiesCNN, RNN, Transformer and LLM, and what each is for.
  • Training and evaluationTrain a model and measure how well it works.

Research

Ask a question, test it properly and write it up.

  • Research questionPick a problem worth investigating.
  • ExperimentsDesign and run a fair test.
  • AnalysisRead your results honestly.
  • Final projectTurn the research into a finished project.
