Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what applicants ask most.

See all questions
Is prior programming experience required?

No. There is no practical examination, and instructors introduce the fundamentals from the beginning of the program.

Is a portfolio required?

Not mandatory, though recommended. Computer-related work receives additional consideration during review.

What should a portfolio include?

Academic record, extracurricular activities, projects, and competition results. Computer-related work is given priority.

How many students are admitted per cohort?

Approximately 25 students: around 10 seats reserved for internal (PSM) students and 15 for external applicants.

How does this differ from the Computer Science major?

The curriculum emphasizes applied, hands-on learning, from foundational engineering and programming to real-world problem solving with robotics and microcontrollers, including coursework certified by FIBO, KMUTT.

What activities are offered?

A wide range, including study visits, workshops, and participation in robotics and project competitions.

Where do graduates go next?

Over 72% of alumni are admitted through TCAS Round 1, most continuing into engineering faculties.
